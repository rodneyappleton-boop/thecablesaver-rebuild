Questions

Frequently asked questions

Does AccuView work on my Fire TV Stick?

Older Fire TV Sticks work — the original Fire TV Stick, Lite, 4K, 4K Max, 4K Plus and Fire TV Cube all run Amazon's Android-based Fire OS. Amazon's newest sticks (the Fire TV Stick 4K Select and 2026 models) run a new system called Vega OS and are not compatible. To check yours: go to Settings → My Fire TV → About. If the software version starts with "1.", it's a new model and won't work. Not sure? Call 1-888-988-8353 and we'll confirm.

What is AccuView TV?

An internet-based streaming service that provides live television channels, live sports, local stations, and premium movie channels over your high-speed internet connection — without cable cords, satellite dishes, or cable boxes.

How much does it cost? Any hidden fees?

A flat $39.95 per month. No broadcast surcharges, no sports network fees, and no equipment rental charges.

Do I need cable to use AccuView?

No. AccuView replaces cable completely. You need a reliable high-speed internet connection and a compatible streaming device.

What internet speed do I need?

We recommend at least about 20 Mbps download for smooth HD on one device, and more if several people stream at once. These are guidelines, not a guarantee of uninterrupted playback.

Which other devices are compatible?

Supported Android-based streaming devices, including our recommended onn Google TV devices. You can also watch on Android phones and tablets, iPhones, iPads, and PCs. Not every smart TV is compatible — a supported external device may be required.

Do I have to buy an onn device?

No. If you already own a compatible device — including an older Fire TV Stick — you don't need to buy anything. The onn options are for people who need a new device.

Does it include local channels?

AccuView includes major local broadcast networks and regional affiliate feeds across the U.S. Specific station availability may vary by location.

Can I watch live sports, including pro and college football?

Yes — pro and college football, basketball, baseball, hockey, UFC/MMA, boxing, motorsports, soccer and major events. Availability of specific events or networks should be confirmed before subscribing.

Does it include premium movie channels?

Yes. Premium movie channels with hit movies, series, and family programming are included in the $39.95 monthly subscription.

How do I install it, and how fast can I start?

After subscribing you receive your login and setup instructions. Install the approved app on your device, sign in, and start watching. Step-by-step visuals are at accuviewsetupguide.com.

Is there a contract or credit check?

No. It's month-to-month with no credit check. Cancel anytime with no penalties.

How do I cancel?

Manage or cancel any time through your payment account or by contacting customer support before your next billing cycle.

What is the refund policy?

7-Day Money-Back Guarantee for first-time subscribers, requested within seven calendar days of the initial purchase. Renewals are non-refundable and there are no prorated refunds. Contact 1-888-988-8353 or accuviewtv@gmail.com with your PayPal transaction ID and checkout email.

Can I use it on multiple TVs?

Yes, across devices in your home according to your subscription's connection allowance.
